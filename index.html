30-second response

Missed calls texted back instantly

The second a call goes unanswered — day or after hours — the lead gets a friendly text and a booking link, so you're first in the conversation, not fourth.

Follow-up that actually happens

Booking on autopilot

Inspections book & remind themselves

Leads pick a slot from a live calendar and get automatic confirmations, 24-hour and 2-hour reminders, plus self-service reschedule — fewer no-shows, zero phone tag.

  • Real-time calendar the lead books directly
